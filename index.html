Listen Reader

EPUBs as clean pages for Safari’s Listen to Page. Books stay on this device.

  • Built-in player (▶): follows the text, skips by paragraph and carries on into the next chapters. Pick a Premium voice under Voice & options.
  • Safari’s Listen to Page (Aa menu): keeps going with the screen locked, but stops at the end of each page. Tap Next, then start it again.
  • Tap any paragraph to read from there, or to start a new page there for Listen to Page.
